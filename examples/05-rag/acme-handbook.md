# Acme Corp customer handbook (fictional sample data)

## Returns and refunds
Customers may return any product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused.

## Shipping
Orders ship within 2 business days from the Springfield warehouse. Express shipping arrives the next business day.

## Warranty
All Acme hardware carries a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.

## Support
Support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00-17:00. Report security issues to security@example.com.
